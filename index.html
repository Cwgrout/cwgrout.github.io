Food Insecurity Analysis Across US States

Analyzing the relationship between food insecurity and poverty rates

Problem: Understanding the distribution of food insecurity across US states and its correlation with poverty rates [1].

What I did: Analyzed food insecurity data for all US states, examining rates ranging from 9% in North Dakota to 19.4% in Arkansas, alongside poverty percentages from 2022 [1].

What I found: States with highest food insecurity include Arkansas (19.4%), Kentucky (18.8%), Louisiana (17.7%), and Texas (17.6%). There appears to be correlation between poverty rates and food insecurity levels [1].

What I would do differently: Include temporal analysis to track changes over time, add demographic breakdowns, and incorporate additional socioeconomic factors beyond poverty rates.

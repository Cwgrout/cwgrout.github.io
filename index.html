01 / Social geography · April 2026

Food insecurity & poverty
across the United States

An interactive comparison of food insecurity and poverty at state and city scales, exploring where these challenges overlap and where their patterns differ.

ArcGIS StoryMapsBivariate mappingData visualization
Project overview
  • Question: How do food insecurity and poverty vary across U.S. states and cities?
  • Approach: Combine interactive maps, population context, and comparison charts.
  • Takeaway: The two measures often track together, but regional differences deserve closer attention.

02 / Urban mobility · April 2026

Taxi activity during
the 2016 NYC blizzard

A look at how a major winter storm changed taxi usage in New York City, comparing activity before, during, and after the January 2016 blizzard.

ArcGIS StoryMapsTemporal analysisComparison maps
Project overview
  • Question: How did the January 2016 blizzard affect NYC taxi usage?
  • Approach: Compare mapped changes, Manhattan neighborhood charts, and hourly activity in a calendar heat chart.
  • Focus: Explore spatial and temporal differences around a major weather event.
